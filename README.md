# Your Only Move Is Hustle Tournament Client

## What is this?
This repository is an API & godot 3.5 mod for the steam version of a videogame known as Your Only Move Is Hustle(colloquially refered to as yomih or yomi) by Ivy Sly. The purpose of this mod and API is to provide tournament organisers and yomih players with a smoother less interupted tournament hosting and playing expierience. It's development was built out of frustration with the issues of Challonge, Start.gg, and just running a tournament in general. 

## My Issues with the Current Solution.
This following paragraph is just me explaining my grievences with these tournament management websites so if you are reading to understand how to help contribute or use this software scroll on. I, snage, used to run a modded tournament server known as Modded Mountain. When I ran it I used mainly challonge, and when I had more free time I would have people register either by pinging me to add them to the list or via challonge's signup link. Yomih is a game that can take anywhere from 20 - 80 mins to finish a singular game, depending on both players skill. This would lead to tournaments taking around 3 - 4 hours to manage start to end, now this work wasn't that difficult it was mostly reexplaining to people the rules or how I ran things, the other major part was updating the bracket. My thorough process of wanting to make sure someone couldn't lie and cause undue stress and delays required a replay sent after the match as a source of truth to refer to in the case of shenanigans. So from a player perspective after playing a match they would send or record their replay, send the video in discord, and then update their scores on a website that requires an account. Or they could just post the replay and tell me who won and I would update the bracket for them. This system is jank, the introduction of any website for managing simply "move my name up" and "you fight XYZ next". Not to mention when the website has technical difficulties or a player misuses the site requiring a tournament organizer to step in and fix it manually. It leads to a stressful and frustrating workflow that is the main driving force on me NOT wanting to run a tournament for this fun community.

## My Purposed Solution
An ingame client that manages the bracket and all the logic it entails along with also letting players to: 
- Post their own tournaments
- Register & Browse tournaments with ease
- Report on their match with their replay and update the bracket almost instantly after a game
- Check in to tournaments
- Watch a tournament through replays
And so the general idea of a window that provides these functions to players is made.
